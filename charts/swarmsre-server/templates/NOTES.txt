Thank you for installing {{ .Chart.Name }}.

To access the SwarmSRE Control Plane dashboard:

1. Forward the port from the service to your local machine:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ .Chart.Name }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

2. Open your browser and navigate to:
   http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Note: SwarmSRE runs in a unified architecture where both the FastAPI backend and React frontend are served from the same container.
